# Level 1 Challenge 97: Love Triangle Beatings (Crush of Crush)

## Problem Description
The students of college XYZ are getting jealous of the students of college ABC because ABC managed to beat XYZ in all sports and games events. The main strength of ABC is their unity. The geeks of XYZ prepared a special perfume making anyone who inhales it extremely violent.

There are $N$ boys $(1, 2, \dots, N)$ and $N$ girls $(1, 2, \dots, N)$ in ABC college. Each boy has a crush on a single girl, and each girl has a crush on a single boy. Every student decides to beat up his/her crush's crush:
- If boy $x$ has a crush on girl $y$ and girl $y$ has a crush on boy $z$, boy $x$ will beat boy $z$ up (provided $x \ne z$).
- If girl $a$ has a crush on boy $b$ and boy $b$ has a crush on girl $c$, girl $a$ will beat girl $c$ up (provided $a \ne c$).

The college doctor needs answers to 2 questions:
1. What is the number of beatings received by the worst-case patient(s)?
2. What is the total number of pairs of students who ended up beating up each other?

## Input Format
- The first line consists of $T$, the number of test cases.
- Each test case consists of 3 lines:
  - First line: integer $N$.
  - Second line: $N$ space-separated integers representing the crush of boy $i$.
  - Third line: $N$ space-separated integers representing the crush of girl $i$.

## Output Format
For every test case, on a new line, print two space-separated integers: the maximum beatings received by any single student followed by the number of mutual beating pairs.

## Constraints
- $1 \le T \le 10$
- $1 \le N \le 10^5$
